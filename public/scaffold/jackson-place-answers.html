Question two · the school in the room

The zoned school is a starting point. Richmond is a choice district.

The site's zoned elementary is George W. Carver Elementary, Richmond Public Schools. In Richmond, the zoned school is not a sentence. Through Enroll RPS open enrollment, a family is not restricted to their residential zone school. Buyers should confirm the current attendance zone for a specific address with RPS, since zones change.

We do not grade schools, and we are not allowed to. Most agents either dodge this question or answer it with an opinion they cannot legally give. We answer it with the choice map and the sources: buyers check Niche.com, GreatSchools.org, and the Virginia School Quality Profiles and decide for themselves.

Open enrollment runs annually through Enroll RPS. Placement is by randomized lottery and transportation is not provided for out-of-zone seats. This describes a public process open to every buyer on identical terms.
