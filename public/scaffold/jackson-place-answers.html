Question two · the school in the room

The zoned school is where you start. In Richmond, it is not where you are stuck.

The zoned elementary is George W. Carver Elementary, and its state scores have climbed three years running: reading from 20 to 34 percent, math from 8 to 30 percent (VDOE, 2022 to 2025). But Richmond runs open enrollment, so a Jackson Place family is not assigned and done. They rank up to three schools outside their zone, plus one more for K to 5, and a lottery places them.
