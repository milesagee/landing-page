Section 04 · How the partnership works

You travel. I close. We share.

Four moves on a loop. You live your life on purpose. I work the business around it. Value flows back so the trips pay for themselves.

01
You travel + scout
Go where you would go anyway. Note the Airbnbs you would love to actually stay at. Note the wineries you want to spend an afternoon at. Note the parks you want to take the kids to.
02
You send me the leads
A text, a screenshot, a name. That is the whole handoff. You do not have to write anything. The thinking has already happened by the time you have noticed.
03
I close with a proposal
I run the same kind of branded dashboard pitch I am running with hosts right now. The host gets a real proposal, not a DM. I do the work. You do not pitch anyone.
04
Value flows back
In-kind hosting covers the stay. Travel and meals are on me. The rest is the part we talk about together, in the callout below.
The economics

The Airbnb covers itself. The travel covers itself. Anything the proposals produce beyond that, we split in a way we both feel good about. We talk numbers on Sunday.
