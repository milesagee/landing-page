The Frame

The goal is one feeling. The buyer walks in and the house feels brighter, warmer, and more intentional than every other Huguenot listing they walked through that weekend.

Section 01 · The Light Read

Cedar Grove drinks light. That changes the rules.

Your block has mature hardwoods. The canopy filters every south-facing window. That means anything below LRV 60 will read dull in the halls and secondary bedrooms.

The lighter, warmer end of the palette wins here. We're looking for LRV 64 or higher as the whole-house standard. Anything darker becomes a single-room moment, not a default.

That's the call behind every pick below. Brightness first. Warmth second. Trend third. In that order.

Section 02 · The Whole-House Picks

Three that hold the house. And feel current.

Each one carries entry, living, dining, halls, and bedrooms without ever entering the overused gray family. Ranked.

Pick 01 · The Default
SW Shoji White
#E5DFCE LRV 74 Warm white
Brightens a wooded lot without going sterile. Warms the brick and hardwood context the block was built on. Trim in SW Alabaster. Ceiling in SW Pure White.
Pick 02 · The Warmer Read
SW Natural Linen
#DFD3C3 LRV 66 Soft linen
More character than a warm white. None of the cool gray cast. The Richmond-specific answer for anyone who's tired of the agreeable gray era but doesn't want stark.
Pick 03 · The Brightest
BM Swiss Coffee
#EEE9E1 LRV 82 Warm white
Use this if the house feels darker than expected on the walkthrough. Highest light bounce on the list. Photographs cleaner than anything in the gray family ever has.
Section 03 · If We Get Bolder

One room. One statement.

Sherwin-Williams 2026 Color of the Year. Use it in the entry or dining if we want one room to land harder than the rest. Not in halls. Not in shaded bedrooms.

Statement Pick · Main Areas Only
SW Universal Khaki
#B8A992 LRV 40 Sandy khaki
The most current 2026 signal we have. Earthy, grounded, modern without being trendy. Pair with Alabaster trim. Use in one well-lit room only.
Rule: never the halls, never the shaded bedrooms.
Section 04 · The Primary Bedroom

Calm. Premium. Never bold.

The primary bedroom is where 2026 buyers want to feel a deep breath. A soft sage hits that note without becoming a color statement.

Accent Pick · Primary Bedroom
BM October Mist
#B6B8A5 LRV 47 Soft sage
Connects to the 2026 nature-grounded movement without being green-loud. Reads as a softened neutral, not a statement. Pair with White Dove trim. Use full-room or feature wall.
