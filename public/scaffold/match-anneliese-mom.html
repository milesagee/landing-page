Section 04 · The Areas, Plainly

Two reads to start. Suburban-convenient, close to the city.

A quick, factual picture of the top areas on your map, framed for someone moving in fresh. These are starting points, and we sharpen them the moment we know which homes she reacts to.

Midlothian
Chesterfield County · 23112 / 23113
A wide suburban corridor southwest of the city, roughly 20 to 30 minutes to downtown Richmond. Known for established subdivisions and a deep run of everyday retail along Midlothian Turnpike and Route 288.
  • Grocery within a five-minute drive across most of the area (Kroger, Publix, Wegmans nearby).
  • Dining clusters at Westchester Commons and Village of Midlothian.
  • Broad mix of price points, so $325K to $350K is workable here.
Brandermill
Chesterfield County · 23112
A planned community wrapped around Swift Creek Reservoir, inside the Midlothian area. Mature tree cover, walking and cycling paths, and a community-association structure, so the HOA is worth reading closely before an offer.
  • Sundial Market and Commons at Brandermill put groceries and restaurants a few minutes out.
  • Miles of connected paths, so walkability is real inside the community.
  • An HOA that funds the amenities, one of the details I will translate line by line.
Glen Allen
Henrico County · 23059 / 23060 · Near you
North of the city in Henrico, and the one that keeps her closest to you. Here is the straight talk: at a $350K ceiling, active inventory that also hits three-bed, two-bath, 1,600-plus is essentially nonexistent in Glen Allen right now, which is why the homes above came from Chesterfield and closer-in Henrico instead. If the number can flex up, Glen Allen opens up fast, and I will pull it the day it does.
  • Short Pump Town Center and West Broad Street put a large concentration of dining and grocery within a five-minute drive of much of the area.
  • Highway access on two sides, I-64 and I-295, keeps the rest of Richmond close.
  • If schools ever come up, I will point you to Niche and GreatSchools for the current data rather than characterize them myself.
