The verdict

$1.9M is the market's middle. $2.0M is the market's ceiling. Both are defensible, and you already knew it.

At 5,983 square feet, $1,900,000 prices at $317.57 a foot. The resale median in your boundary is $317.52. You land on it to the penny. $2,000,000 prices at $334.28, which is 99.1 percent of the highest price any resale has ever achieved inside these lines. Holding the range instead of narrowing it was the right call, and the data confirmed it after you made it, not before.

The line you cannot cross: every listing that asked $362.70 a foot or more expired unsold, median 176 days. Every listing priced between $300 and $337 sold, median 7 days. There is no slow middle here. This market either takes a house in a week or never takes it at all.

The three that matter more than the medians

Your best comp, and their opening move

Dominion View. Built 2001 to our 2002. 5,934 square feet to our 5,983. Five bedrooms to our five. Expect Ransom's agent to open here, because it is the first thing any competent agent finds.

The ceiling is lower than it looks

$337.33, not $378.92. The one sale above the ceiling was a new build.

12324 Haybrook sold at $378.92 a foot, which looks like headroom until you notice it was built in 2022. New construction carries a premium a 2002 resale does not get, no matter how good the renovation is. Price against $337.33 and treat $378.92 as somebody else's number.

The cautionary tale and the whole competitive field

12554 Kain Rd is the only other home for sale, and it has already failed once.

It asked $2,499,000, which is $362.70 a foot, and expired after 114 days. It came back 18 percent lower at $2,060,000 and is still sitting at 42 days. That is 156 days across two attempts. It is simultaneously your only competition and the clearest proof of what reaching costs in this market.

5701 Ardington Blvd, built 2009, 5,087 square feet, sold at $337.33 a foot in seven days at 99 percent of list. It was missing from the first export. $2.0M sits at the ceiling, not past it, and that makes your range stronger than I said this morning.

Four traps in the raw export

All four are live in the PDFs. Every number on this page is computed around them. Do not quote the export's own averages.

  • BRTVAHN2000992 is 11904 Lerade Ct a second time, double listed in Bright MLS alongside CVR 2519802. Every "Avg for all" line in your exports double counts that sale. This generalizes: CVR pulls can carry cross-MLS duplicates, so dedupe by address before trusting any blended figure.
  • 6043 Corwin sold at 118 percent of list, $1,295,950 asked and $1,530,000 paid. That is a listing error, not market evidence, and it drags the bottom of the band down artificially.
  • 13009 Huntsteed Ct is a broken listing, not a market signal. It expired twice for 481 total days, once after the seller raised the price mid-listing. It is the only expire under $362 and it should come out of the cliff read entirely.
  • The final pull filtered to $1.9M and up, so expireds below $1.9M are invisible. That is the right frame for our price point, but it means you cannot claim nothing failed under that number.

What gates the send

His page carries no internal bar and nothing to strip. It is send-ready as a file. These two are the reasons I would still not send it today.

Blocker one

Ten exterior frames. No primary, no bath, no bedroom, no kitchen.

He already saw a rough draft. The next version cannot be a $1.9M home with no interior photography. I pulled the line that announced the gap, because narrating it made it worse, but pulling the line did not close it. This is a Cess request, not a data problem, and it is the single largest thing standing between this page and a send.

Blocker two

The Pool row came out, and it was our strongest row.

I could not verify whether either Hanover comp has a pool, and every IDX site blocks automated reads. A finished comparison table has no blank cells, so the row came out rather than shipping three "not confirmed" gaps in front of him. Pull the two Hanover MLS sheets and Pool goes straight back in, almost certainly as a clean win for us.

Still open

  • Do the three Dominion View sales have golf frontage and pools? A sixty second remarks read in CVR. If 11905 Lerade at $318.88 is a non-golf, non-pool house, our defensible premium over it is materially larger than anything currently claimed on the buyer page.
  • Pool, pavilion, third floor and main level bedroom on the two Hanover comps. They sit outside every export and every IDX site blocks automated reads, so this needs the Hanover MLS sheets.
  • GPIN, Henrico assessment, HOA dues and renovation year.
  • Interior photos of the bedroom levels. Nine exterior frames and no primary, no bath, no bedroom. At this number that is the largest single gap on the buyer page.
