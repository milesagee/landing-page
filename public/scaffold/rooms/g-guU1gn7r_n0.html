Dad. Ask me three things first.

Then tell me whatever you were going to tell me. I will take all of it.

You taught me how to look at something and tell what is wrong with it. The first move was never the opinion. It was figuring out what the thing was built to do. That is the only thing I am asking for here, and I am asking for it before I say anything, not after.

Before you say anything
  1. What are you actually trying to do here?
  2. What have you already tried that I don't know about?
  3. Do you and I want the same thing?

Ask me these out loud. Then say whatever you were going to say.

Why I am asking

It is usually not the part you heard.

Something I say is going to bother you. It might even be right. But it is usually not the piece that made you feel the way you are about to feel. You hear one part, the part lands wrong, and the whole rest of it gets answered as if that part was the point.

So ask me the three above before you say the thing. Especially the third one. If the answer is yes, and it is yes, then whatever you tell me after that is coming from somebody on my side, and I will hear it completely differently. So will you.

To be clear

What I am not asking.

I am not asking you to go easy on me. You are the one person who tells me when something is off, and you are usually right. I am asking you to grade the real thing instead of the piece of it that reached you first.
