2

The standard we hold.

Four things. They apply whether you're on a MAMS buyer or an AG seller, whether you're drafting an email or sitting on a hard call. This is the part that doesn't change between businesses.

One

Do the thing nobody asked for.

The bar isn't "handled it." The bar is: would this make them feel genuinely seen? The unexpected, unrequested move is what people remember and tell other people about.

Two

Run the lens, not the script.

Scripts get you to the right shape. They don't make you good. We're after fluency, the standard living in your bones, so you can read the room and choose the right move instead of reciting one.

Three

Confidence and transparency.

We never fight for our value, we show it. Be direct, be specific, tell the truth even when it's the uncomfortable part. People trust the person who isn't selling them.

Four

Nothing falls through the cracks.

Every lead has a next step. Every client feels remembered. The follow-through is the whole game. The standard is that nobody on our watch ever goes quiet because we forgot them.
