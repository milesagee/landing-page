Scott's Addition

ZZQ Texas Craft Barbeque

If your boys only eat one thing here, make it this. Central Texas method, brisket by the pound, and it sells out most days well before close. Get there by noon or plan something else.

OpenWednesday to Sunday, 11am to 6pm, unless sold out
Where3201 West Moore Street
First come, first served. No reservations, and sold out means sold out.
zzqrva.com

Ardent Craft Ales

The beer garden is the move for a group. Open seven days, big outdoor space, and they invite you to bring in food from neighbors like ZZQ. That combination is the single easiest afternoon on this list.

OpenMon to Wed 12 to 9pm, Thu and Fri 12 to 10pm, Sat 11am to 10pm, Sun 12 to 8pm

Mama J's

Richmond soul food, and the answer when somebody asks what the city actually tastes like. Fried catfish, candied yams, the whole thing. Expect a wait at peak and take it.

OpenDaily. Exact hours are not posted online, so call ahead at (804) 225-7449
Where415 North 1st Street
mamajskitchen.com

Jewish delicatessen downtown, and the right call for a group breakfast before anybody has made a decision about the day. Kitchen closes at 3pm, so this is a morning play only.

Church Hill

Sub Rosa Bakery

This is the one that makes you look like you actually live here. Wood fired oven, grain milled on site, in Church Hill rather than the neighborhood every visitor gets pointed to. Go early, the good stuff goes.

OpenTue to Fri 7am to 5pm, Sat and Sun 8am to 5pm
ClosedMondays
Where620 North 25th Street
subrosabakery.com
James River Park System

Belle Isle and the Pipeline

Class III rapids inside the city limits, which almost nowhere else in the country has. Belle Isle by the footbridge under the Lee Bridge, the Pipeline Overlook for the skyline shot your boys will actually post.

Water4 to 5.5 feet is the good range. Above 6 feet the hazards go up sharply
SafetyThe park says life jackets are strongly recommended at every water level, swift currents and unknown depths
CheckWestham gauge, the morning you go
This one number decides whether it is a great day or a bad idea. Check it before you load the car.
Live river level, Westham gauge
jamesriverpark.org
