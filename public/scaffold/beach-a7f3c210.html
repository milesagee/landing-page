The rule that decides everything

Virginia Beach will not let most of these houses rent.

Most agents would hand you twenty one listings and let you sort it out. Here is the part that eliminates almost all of them.

The city zoning code lists renting by the week as prohibited in every residential district. Not difficult. Not discretionary. Prohibited. There are exactly two exceptions: Sandbridge, and a narrow strip of the Oceanfront that stops at 42nd Street.

This is the part people get wrong. Outside those two areas there is no permit to apply for. A beautiful house in Bay Colony or Princess Anne Hills can be bought. It can never be rented out by the week, no matter who you ask or what you offer.

Which is why two listings here matter more than the other nineteen. Both are already licensed and already renting. You would be buying a business that is already running, not asking permission and hoping.

The good news, and it is the most important sentence on this page. Virginia Beach code section 241.2 says a grandfathered short term rental status runs with the land. It transfers to you when you buy. What does not transfer is the annual permit and the tax registration, which you re apply for in your own name.

So here is the one thing I will not let you skip. Before a dollar is at risk, the seller produces the current zoning permit, the conditional use permit or the pre 2018 registration, and the lodging tax filings. In writing. A listing that says it is licensed is a claim, not proof.

One more thing worth knowing. The city is right now considering shrinking the Oceanfront district. The planning commission recommended it eleven to nothing on August 12. City Council has not voted, and the next chances are October 6 and October 20. Neither of these two houses is in the piece being cut, but this is a moving target and I am watching it.

So you can picture it

Virginia Beach, in Richmond terms.

Think of the Oceanfront strip as Broad Street. Rudee Inlet to 89th Street is about five miles, so Oceanfront to North End is Shockoe Bottom to Willow Lawn. Bay Colony, Princess Anne Hills and Linkhorn Park sit one block inland the way Windsor Farms sits behind Cary Street. Chic's Beach is Short Pump to the Fan, about 17 minutes. Sandbridge is the Fan to Chester, about 21 minutes from the Oceanfront on a two-lane road with nothing between.

One thing worth sitting with. Almost every beach neighborhood here costs more per square foot than Windsor Farms, on much smaller lots. That is the tax on being near the water.

The part your map left out

There is one place in this city where the answer is just yes.

The circle you drew runs from Chic's Beach down to Rudee Inlet. It stops right above the one neighborhood where renting by the week is allowed by right, with no permit to beg for and no council vote to survive. Sandbridge.

In Richmond terms, Sandbridge is the Fan to Chester. About twenty five minutes south of the Oceanfront on a single two lane road. And I am going to be straight with you about what that costs you.

Now the part nobody puts in the brochure. Sandbridge has no boardwalk. There is no walking to dinner. It is a handful of restaurants and a small market, and a real grocery run is about fifteen minutes back up the road. That road floods, and the civic league president says it closes about once a week even in winter.

And $1,500,000 does not buy oceanfront there. Sand in the back yard starts around $2,100,000. At your number you are buying across the street from the ocean, not on it.

So here is the trade, plainly. Sandbridge is the only place your income plan is legal without a fight. It is also the one place that does not give you the boardwalk walk you both described loving. That is a real choice and it is yours, not mine.

Where I land

If you asked me tonight, here is what I would say.

Nothing at this price pays for itself on a loan. Not one house on this page. The rent covers somewhere between forty and fifty five percent of the payment. If the plan only works when the house pays for itself, the honest answer is that the plan needs a bigger down payment or a smaller number, and I would rather tell you that now than after you fall in love with a kitchen.

If you are paying cash, this changes completely. Sandbridge nets about $35,579 a year, 965 Pacific about $25,033, 8502 Ocean Front about $29,895. All three are a real return on a house your family also gets to use.

For the life you described, walking the beach and then walking to dinner, 8502 Ocean Front Ave is the one. It is the only house here that is actually on the sand, and you do not cross a single street to get there. Quiet noise band, already licensed, already furnished. It is small and it is expensive per foot, and I still think you should see it first.

For the income, it is Sandbridge and it is not close. If you will let me widen the map, I will bring you the Sandbridge list next.

And one thing in your favor on both of them. 8502 Ocean Front has been sitting 111 days and already came down $50,000 in July. 965 Pacific has been sitting 132 days. Both of them sat through the entire summer, which is the season a beach house is supposed to sell in, and now they are heading into winter with the listings still open. That is the moment a seller gets reasonable. I would not pay either asking price.

Where these numbers come from. All 21 houses, their prices, bedroom and bath counts, square footage, year built, zoning, tax bills and jet noise readings are pulled directly from the REIN multiple listing service sheets as of September 17, 2026. Nothing on this page is estimated where a real figure exists. The rental rates used for 965 Pacific Ave are the ones the listing agent published: $500 to $650 a night in summer and $3,000 to $3,500 a month in winter.

Where I am estimating, and I will say so. Occupancy, management fees, insurance, utilities, maintenance and the mortgage rate are estimates. Insurance and flood are the biggest unknowns and can move the yearly cost by thousands on a house this close to the water. Those are being verified now and this page will be updated. Rental income for 8502 Ocean Front Ave is estimated from comparable properties because the listing does not publish its rates, though it does state it has a rental history.

The jet noise numbers are real and they matter. Every one of these listings carries an official AICUZ noise reading from Naval Air Station Oceana. Anything above 75 decibels is the loudest band the Navy measures. That affects living there and it affects what guests say about the house afterward.

About the Sandbridge numbers. The income figures for the two Sandbridge houses are quoted from the listings themselves, not verified. Before anyone relies on them they get checked against real rental statements and lodging tax filings. The Sandbridge tax rate of ninety eight cents per hundred is from the city assessor. Flood insurance there is the single biggest unknown on this page, and on an exposed lot it can run past $12,000 a year on its own.
