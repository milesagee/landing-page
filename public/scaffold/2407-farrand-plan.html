3 The two lots

Can it be split? Likely yes, and here is the honest version.

You asked, so here is the straight answer. Henrico requires a new lot to have 50 feet of frontage on the street and a buildable area outside the floodplain. Your lot has the room for that up front, which means a second building lot is likely possible with a survey and county sign-off. No rezoning needed. That is the good news, and it is real.

Here is the honest part, because you deserve it. Tearing anything down to build does not add money for you, the cost of new construction eats the gain. So we do not treat the split as the plan. We treat it as a bonus we mention to the right buyer, a builder or an investor who values the option and will pay a little more for it. It quietly widens who wants your property. It does not replace the sale.

On the Airbnb question: we already checked Henrico's rules so you do not have to. Short-term rentals like Airbnb are only allowed at an owner's primary residence, at least 185 days a year. For a home you are not living in, Henrico does not permit it. Long-term rent is the path, and the numbers above are the real ones.
