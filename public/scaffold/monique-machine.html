06 · Objections

What you'll hear, and how we answer it.

Every one of these has a clean answer. Know them cold and there is nothing on the call that can knock you off balance.

$20K is a lot. ChatGPT is twenty bucks a month.
Different category. ChatGPT cannot read your texts, does not know your pipeline, and forgets you between chats. We are the price of one deal's commission, and it works every single day for years. Then show the daily brief live. The demo is the close.
And I still pay monthly costs on top?
Their accounts, their keys, no markup from us. The spend cap means they control the ceiling. Framed right, it is $25 to $40 a day for a full-time analyst that never clocks out.
What happens when it breaks?
That is what the retainer is for. Maintenance and monitoring are baked in, we can service it remotely without ever touching their data, and the machine we ship is one we fully stand behind.
How do I know my private data is safe?
Because it never leaves the room. The Machine runs on their box, we hold no keys, access unlocks in stages, and there is a kill switch. The local-first design is not a feature we bolt on, it is the whole architecture.
Isn't AI going to make all this free soon?
Every improvement makes their Machine better at no cost to them. They are not buying the model. They are buying the integration into their business and a partner who keeps it sharp. That does not commoditize.
