Watch out · 1 of 2

Most prep buys the vanity that is in stock and finds out about the plumbing later. Right-offset means the sink sits right and the drawers sit left. Center-sink 36-inch vanities are 90 percent of the market and look nearly identical in a photo. Before any checkout, confirm the words "right offset" are on the product page.

Watch out · 2 of 2

94 inches is an installer decision, not a saw cut. The 2 inches come out of the toe-kick recess or a top filler, never the finished box or the door openings. Have the installer confirm which method works in that spot before the cabinet is ordered. If the box cannot absorb the 2 inches, the fallback is a shorter cabinet built up on a base, not a modified Melvern.
