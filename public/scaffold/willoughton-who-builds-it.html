The short version

Richmond Hill designs and develops it. LifeStyle builds it.

On 19 of the 20 new construction permits pulled for their homes, including the permit for this one, the contractor of record is LifeStyle Builders and Developers Inc. 1 Why two names

A joint venture, stated in their own words

Kevin McNulty is owner of LifeStyle Home Builders and is the joint venture partner with Richmond Hill Design + Build. LifeStyle handles all of the vertical construction and warranty for the joint venture. Richmond Hill Design + Build, richmondhillbuilds.com/about-us, read September 1, 2026

The city permit file matches that statement. On the building permit for 2705 Willoughton, BLDR-156979-2025, the contractor of record reads LIFESTYLE BUILDERS AND DEVELOPERS INC and the owner reads Lloyd M Poe Investments Inc.

There is one more piece that explains the whole arrangement. Lloyd Poe founded LifeStyle, ran it for decades, and sold it in 2016. He started Richmond Hill in 2018 with his daughter Vanessa, an architect. So the design company and the construction company are not strangers who found each other. They share a founder.

4 Before you act

Three things I want in writing first

The warranty document, and whose name is on it

No written warranty is published anywhere. Not on Richmond Hill's site, not on the Enclave at Westview site, and not on LifeStyle's site, which has a warranty request portal but states no coverages, exclusions, or durations. Without a document, what you have is the Virginia statutory implied warranty under Code section 55.1-357, and that statute allows a contract to waive it if the waiver is written conspicuously. The seller on the deed will be Lloyd M Poe Investments Inc. The company promising the warranty is LifeStyle. Two different companies, so we get the document and confirm which one honors it.

The certificate of occupancy

As of September 1 the plumbing, electrical, mechanical and gas permits at this address were all finaled between June and August, but the main building permit still read Issued rather than Finaled. That is likely just sequencing on a house going live September 3, and I will confirm it with the city.

One neighboring permit that expired

The building permit at 2716 Willoughton Lane, three lots away, expired June 16, 2026 without a final inspection. The record does not say why. It is a question worth asking, not a conclusion.

5 Honest limits

What could not be checked, and why

  • Virginia state court records. Richmond City and Chesterfield civil filings were not searched. The state court portal's terms prohibit automated searching. These have to be run by hand and I am doing that.
  • Recorded mechanic's liens. Same reason, plus the city's land records moved to a portal that blocks outside requests.
  • Contractor board disciplinary history. The Virginia DPOR lookup sits behind a security challenge that cannot be queried directly. Also being run by hand.
  • Any homeowner review, good or bad. Zero first hand accounts exist on twelve platforms checked, including Houzz, BuildZoom, the BBB, Yelp, Angi, Nextdoor and Reddit. Houzz literally says you could be the first review. That is an absence of published opinions, and it is not evidence about the work in either direction.
