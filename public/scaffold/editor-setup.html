Set Once

The folder structure that scales

Date-prefix everything and label the vertical. The brand kit is what makes a trial editor's work actually look like yours instead of generic.

MAMS Content/ _BRAND KIT/ # logo, fonts, colors, lower-third style, music, caption rules _RAW INBOX/ # dated dump zone for new shoots PROJECTS/ 2026-06-26 [project name]/ 01 RAW/ # original clips + photos 02 WORKING/ # drafts in progress 03 EXPORTS/ # final renders ready to post PUBLISHED/ # what went live + the caption used

The Gauntlet

Give them both tasks

Each one tests a different muscle. You want to see both before you commit a seat.

Raw clips, one Reel

Tests instinct

Hand over a RAW folder plus the brand kit. Brief: make one Reel. No script, no guidance.

Nails it = taste. Story, hook, and pacing are theirs.

Finish to brand

Tests discipline

Hand over a near-done timeline or a script plus clips. Brief: finish it to match our look.

Nails it = discipline. They can match the brand without hand-holding.
One Flag

Keep raws in Drive, not in CapCut

CapCut is ByteDance, and its US availability has wobbled politically before. For a trial week it does not matter. But keeping your raw files in Drive means a CapCut disruption never touches your source footage. Drive is the asset you own; CapCut is just the workbench.
