03
What happens next

In order, so nothing surprises you.

No dates on these yet, on purpose. The sequence is fixed. The timing is a conversation you and I have, not something I decide for you.

  1. Photo selection and sequencing. Which shot leads matters more than how many there are. The first image decides whether anyone sees the second.

  2. Listing copy written. Written to be read by a person, not stuffed with adjectives that every listing already uses.

  3. Story video finished and scheduled. Cut, captioned, and slotted so it lands when the listing does, not a week behind it.

  4. You and I set price and go-live together. One conversation, real numbers, and my honest read even where it is not the fun answer.

  5. Live, everywhere at once. MLS, the syndication sites, and the social push on the same day rather than staggered.

  6. Showing feedback comes back to you. What people actually said, including the criticism. That is the part that tells us whether to hold or adjust.

04
The part you asked about

There is a system running underneath all of it.

This is what I have been building. It is called Monique. It is not a chatbot and it is not an app I bought. It is a machine that reads everything and makes sure nothing falls.

  • Every morning it reads every channel

    Texts, email, calendar, the CRM, and the local market. It sorts what is urgent from what is noise before I have had coffee, and it tells me who is waiting on me.

  • It cues my editors with real direction

    My video team does not guess what to cut. Every morning they get a board that names the piece, the reason it matters this week, and the exact footage to pull. Your house is in that queue.

  • It will not let a person go quiet

    It tracks how long it has been since I actually showed up for someone and tells me when I am slipping. That is the part I am least willing to leave to memory.

  • It builds pages like this one

    This page did not exist an hour ago. That is the point. When something deserves its own surface, it gets one, instead of being flattened into a text message.
