Four things a photo cannot tell us

Two of these hard-gate a category. Buying the wrong side of either one means a return trip or an open wall.

  1. Shower valve brandgates a category

    Trim kits are brand-locked to the rough-in valve. A Delta trim will not go onto a Moen valve without opening the wall.

    How: Pull the existing escutcheon or check the plaster guard. Look for a stamped brand on the valve body.

  2. Vanity top hole spreadgates a category

    Both tops show three holes. 4-inch centerset and 8-inch widespread are different products and they are not interchangeable.

    How: Measure center to center on the two outer holes.

  3. Counts: interior doors, fan locations, switch devices

    Every one of these is a per-unit multiply. The budget totals mean nothing without them.

    How: Walk the house once with a tally. Doors, fan boxes, and every switch and outlet plate.

  4. Switch scope

    Going flat means swapping the devices themselves, not only the plates. Mixed switch styles in one house read unfinished.

    How: Confirm whether existing devices are being replaced or only re-plated.

The metal

One metal, repeated everywhere, is what separates a finished house from a house that got finished in pieces. The quartz has gold in the veining. The floors are red oak. The tile is greige. Three warm votes already on the board. But satin nickel is what is hanging on the doors today, and nickel is never wrong.

Browse at Lowe's

Champagne bronze

Warm, soft, slightly pink gold

Reads the most expensive against warm quartz. Delta and Amerock both carry deep lines, so matching across plumbing and hardware is easy.

2

Satin nickel

Cool, neutral, already installed

Zero swap cost on the levers, vanity light, and showerhead. Never offends anyone. Also never excites anyone.

3

Matte black

High contrast, graphic

Picks up the black front door and reads current. Highest contrast against white quartz, which cuts both ways.

4

Aged brass

Warmest, most editorial

The strongest match to the gold veining and the only option with real personality. Also the one that dates fastest if it goes shiny.
