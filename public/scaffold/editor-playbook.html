The Brand in 30 Seconds

Confident, specific, Richmond-coded.

MAMS is Richmond real estate with a point of view. The audience is people moving here and locals who love it. The brand pushes the wrong people away so the right people lean in. That polarity is the engine. Never sand it into something safe.

The read-aloud test: if the text sounds like Miles talking on TikTok, ship it. If it sounds like a brokerage, rewrite it.
The Hard Rules

Break these and it's off-brand.

No em dashes. Ever. Period, comma, or line break.
No emojis in the on-screen text.
You-frame. Talk to the viewer. Never open on "I".
Contractions always. you're, it's, let's. Never "you are".
Title card = one word or phrase. All caps. Never a sentence.
Land line = 5 to 9 words. Short, punchy, quotable.
Hook in the first 1.5 seconds. Topic and tension, instant.
Captions burned in, beat-timed. No autocaption sludge.
Specificity over warmth. Named streets and real numbers.
Fair housing. Talk about the place, never who lives there. No "good schools", no "family-friendly".
