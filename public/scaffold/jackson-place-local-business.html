The answer

A competitor can hand a buyer a furniture credit. Nobody can hand them Jackson Ward.

Every Jackson Place buyer closes with The 2nd Street Welcome. Roughly $5,000 in credit, spendable at one place on earth: the independent businesses still standing in this neighborhood. Sunday dinner at Mama J's. A bottle from Penny's. A seat at the Hippodrome.

You already named a street for Maggie Walker, who chartered the first bank ever led by a Black woman in America, in 1903, on these blocks. This is what it looks like to mean it.

It also earns the premium. At roughly $307 a foot, Jackson Place sits about 10% over Manchester Square's realized $280, and our own analysis says that spread holds on the address, the story and the spec, not on location. They have a rooftop and a river. So we stop selling location, and we sell the one thing they cannot build across the water.
