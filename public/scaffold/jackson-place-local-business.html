The move

A competitor can hand a buyer a furniture credit. Nobody can hand them Jackson Ward.

Every buyer closes with The 2nd Street Welcome. Roughly $5,000 in credit at the independent businesses within a five minute walk of their front door. Sunday dinner at Mama J's. Lunch at Big Herm's. A bottle from Penny's. Six of them sit on N. 2nd Street, the same frontage as your ten flagship homes.

You already named a street for Maggie Walker, who chartered the first bank ever led by a Black woman in America, in 1903, on these blocks. This is what it looks like to mean it.
